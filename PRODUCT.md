# OVERLEET

## Register

product

## Users

People preparing for Data Science, Machine Learning, Statistics, A/B Testing, LLM, Python and SQL/database interviews. They browse by topic and difficulty, write an answer before revealing the explanation, and record what needs further practice. CV and personal-experience questions are outside the question bank.

## Product Purpose

A public, sourced question bank with original answers, mathematical explanations and self-check rubrics. The agreed first release runs locally with PostgreSQL and publishes its source on GitHub. Guests can practise without authentication; GitHub login synchronises completion status.

## Brand Personality

Direct, focused, precise. Use UK English. Avoid em dashes, inflated claims, invented company provenance and unsupported claims about interview frequency.

## Anti-references

Avoid a promotional landing page, decorative statistics, nested cards, animated introductions and features that distract from answering questions. This is a study workspace with a table and reading panes.

## Design Principles

1. Show the question bank immediately.
2. Keep model answers concealed until requested.
3. Make progress changes explicit and recoverable.
4. Distinguish browser-only drafts from account-synchronised status.
5. Keep sources visible and readable.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support and narrow-screen layouts are required by the accepted plan. Do not use colour as the only indicator of difficulty or status.
